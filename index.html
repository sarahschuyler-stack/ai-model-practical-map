Vision: stop choosing one “winner”

For serious AI systems, the better architecture is often a portfolio. Let inexpensive or breadth-oriented models gather possibilities, use a deeper model to reason over them, introduce an independent judge, and reserve the most expensive frontier model for the hardest unresolved work.

Design principle
Do not pay premium reasoning prices for retrieval.

Use lower-cost scouts to collect evidence and candidate paths.

Do not let the scout be the judge.

Separate generation from evaluation to reduce confirmation loops.

Escalate by uncertainty.

Route ambiguous, contradictory or high-value cases to deeper models.

Measure cost per completed task.

Cheap tokens can become expensive when a model needs many retries or tool calls.
